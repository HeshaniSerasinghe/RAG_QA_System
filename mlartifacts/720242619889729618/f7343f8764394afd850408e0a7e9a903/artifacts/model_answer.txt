Training a new puppy involves several key steps and considerations for both you and your pet. Here's an optimal strategy based on expert advice from professionals like Patricia Scott Burch, author of The Other End of the Leash: Why Dogs Have Behavior Problems and What To Do About Them; dog trainer Pippa Dekiel; and applied animal behaviorist, trainer, & consultant Sofie Jl de Bruin.

1. **Establish a Routine** - Consistent daily routines provide your puppy with structure and security which can help manage their energy levels effectively throughout the day or night when it comes to housebreaking (potty training). Set fixed times for meals, walks outside/exercise sessions as well as playtime activities so that they know what's coming next; this could greatly reduce anxiety during sleep and relaxation periods too!

2. **Positive Reinforcement** - Reward good behavior immediately using treats or praises rather than scolding bad actions because punishing mistakes doesn’t teach better responses instead creates fear which makes learning challenges more difficult in future stages (if not done correctly). Treat them as soon after doing something desired so that these behaviors become associated with positive experiences leading to repetition across various environments/situations over time through repeated exposure & application without fail every single day until ingrained habit pattern develops well enough for independence someday..

3. **Potty Training** - Take your puppy out frequently during the first few weeks because accidents happen often especially when starting something new like this so give yourself ample time along with appropriate supplies such as pee pads inside house until they gain control outside itself firmly established habit after mastering basic concept initially learned early days..

4. **Consistency** - Make sure that everyone in the household follows through on training and rules set forth by you/your family members regarding walks or crate times etc., as mixed messages lead towards confusion & lackadaisical compliance hence why consistency across all adults involved becomes vital here making progress smoother along way..

5. **Professional Guidance** - Engage in dog training classes if possible because learning commands such Sit/Down come together faster with guidance from expert trainers who understand breed-specific temperaments & behavioral tendencies towards obedience skills needed ultimately resulting less stressful journey throughout process overall..

6. **Socialization and Exposure** - Introduce your puppy early on different environments like new people, sounds/noises unfamiliar situations etc., slowly exposing them little-by-little which assists building confidence level naturally without fear setting in later stages due lack experience regarding various encounters faced during growing up phase within family circle..

7. **Consistency with Rewards** - Make sure not to punish mistakes but focus on reward systems instead since it builds trust bonding between owner-pet relationship overall ensuring better long term compliance while avoiding developing fearful behavior later in life based solely off negative reinforcement approaches..

8. **Patience and Time** - Remember, training takes time; expect progress at your puppy's own pace without rushing things until they truly understand each command learned thoroughly ensuring maximum retention capacity within memory banks ultimately leading towards successful independent living one day down the road away from constant supervision..
