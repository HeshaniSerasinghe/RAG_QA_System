LoRA stands for Low-Rank Adaptation, which is a parameter optimization method specifically designed to mitigate catastrophic forgetting during neural network training. It does so by introducing low-rank matrices that are added into the input projection layers of pretrained models (like LLAMA) without modifying their original parameters extensively. These additional matrices capture task-specific knowledge, allowing a model fine-tuned on new tasks to retain previous learning while adapting for better performance on those tasks. It essentially helps in updating only certain weights within the encoder projection layers of an LMM (Large Multimodal Model) when tackling sequential finetuning across different target tasks, making it efficient and less prone to overfitting compared to traditional fine-tuning techniques that might heavily update all parameters.
