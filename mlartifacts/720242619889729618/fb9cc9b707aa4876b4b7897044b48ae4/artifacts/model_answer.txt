A Transformer is an architecture originally designed for natural language processing tasks that has gained popularity due to its ability to handle sequential data, such as text or time series information. Unlike traditional recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs), which process inputs in sequence order, a Transformer uses self-attention mechanisms allowing it to weigh the importance of different parts of the input simultaneously across all positions. This parallel processing helps with understanding long-range dependencies within data sequences without requiring sequential computation, leading to more efficient training and better performance on tasks like machine translation and text generation.
